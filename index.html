The trips

Each one comes with three ways to do it. The highlighted plan matches the week style you picked above.

Tickets: no self-transfers

Three one-way tickets: Paris → HK on 24 Oct (from €654), HK → your destination on 31 Oct, and destination → Paris or Brussels on 7 Nov. Every flight home on this page is a single ticket, bag included unless noted, so if a leg runs late the airline rebooks you.

A Paris ⇄ HK return (€1,089) plus a cheap hop back to HK on 7 Nov would mean changing tickets in HK on the way home, so it's left out.

Things to sort this week

  1. Pick the trip and style (this page, or the spreadsheet's My plan tab).
  2. Recheck the forecast about 3 days before you fly. Forecasts 10 or more days out only show trends.
  3. Book Paris → HK, the HK → destination flight and the flight home, each as one ticket. Low-cost fares include a 7 kg cabin bag only; a checked bag adds about €25–45.
  4. Book the scarce beds first: Kumano inns, Koyasan temple, the Shirakawa-go farmhouse, Murodo, island inns on 1–3 Nov.
  5. Book the big activity with free cancellation, since paragliding depends on the wind.
  6. Check your insurance covers paragliding.
  7. Entry: Visit Japan Web, Taiwan arrival card. Nothing needed for Vietnam with a French passport.
  8. Get an eSIM for your destination.
  9. Sun 8 Nov: from CDG, the TGV to Lille Europe takes about an hour; from Brussels Airport, the train via Bruxelles-Midi takes about 1h30 (€25–40).
