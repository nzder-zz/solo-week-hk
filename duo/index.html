TyphoonIn-fa (No. 31 in Japan, 煙花 in Taiwan) reaches the Ogasawara islands, far south of Tokyo, on 31 Oct and slows down there until about 4 Nov; its track after that is uncertain. It is not expected to hit mainland Japan directly, but its moist air may make rain heavier after 4 Nov (tenki.jp, 30 Oct), which falls on your solo days. Taiwan's weather agency expects no effect on Taiwan's weather, only long swells on the east coast (Taitung, Green Island, Lanyu) around 1–3 Nov: no swimming or rock-walking at the waterline in Dulan.
